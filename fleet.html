MEDIUM LOADS

More room for bigger moves

When a compact cargo vehicle is not enough, a pickup or mini truck can provide extra room for furniture, business stock, appliances and heavier packages. Choose based on the complete load, not just the number of items.

Pickup truck

Open cargo space is useful for bulky goods, building supplies, packaged stock and items that are easier to load from the side or rear. Share approximate dimensions and whether the goods need weather protection or securing.

  • Useful for bulky or irregular-shaped loads
  • Suitable for local business and supply runs
  • Confirm load securing and cover requirements

Mini truck for household and shop moves

A practical option for several cartons, furniture pieces, appliances or regular shop replenishment. It can suit customers who need an enclosed-style cargo body or more organized loading space, depending on the available vehicle.

  • Useful for small home shifting jobs
  • Good for cartons, appliances and store stock
  • Confirm vehicle dimensions and carrying limits
Before choosing

List the largest item, estimate total load size, note stairs or lift access and share any narrow entry points at pickup or delivery.

CHOOSE WITH CONFIDENCE

Match capacity to the job.

Consider the size, weight and access conditions at pickup and drop. A compact vehicle may suit smaller cartons and short local trips, while larger loads may need a mini truck or tempo with more cargo space.

Before booking, estimate the number of items, mention bulky pieces and note narrow streets, basement access or loading restrictions. Final vehicle availability and carrying limits are confirmed during booking.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Share a few useful details

Accurate cargo information helps identify a suitable vehicle and ensures smooth pickup and delivery.

STEP 01
CARGO SPECIFICATION

Load size & volume

Approximate count of cartons, furniture pieces, appliances, and fragile or heavy machinery requiring special positioning.

  • ✓ Estimated box count & largest item dimensions
  • ✓ Fragile items or weather cover requirement
Typical reference: 1 BHK (~15 Boxes + Sofa / Bed)
STEP 02
LOCATION ACCESS

Route & building access

Street width at pickup and drop locations, service elevator access vs stair carry, floor number, and basement height clearances.

  • ✓ Elevator availability & dedicated service lift
  • ✓ Narrow street access or parking bay permit
Typical reference: 3rd Floor (Service Lift Available)
STEP 03
SCHEDULE & DISPATCH

Timing & loading window

Preferred moving date, morning or evening slot, and loading buffer time to avoid peak urban traffic or commercial no-entry zones.

  • ✓ Preferred pickup hour & loading buffer
  • ✓ City peak-hour or night entry considerations
Typical reference: Morning 8:00 AM – 10:30 AM Slot
