OUR JOURNEY

Built around the everyday need to move things well.

MoveFleet is shaped around a simple idea: arranging a local cargo vehicle should feel clear and manageable. Customers need to explain what is moving, where it is going and when it should arrive without getting lost in complicated steps.

Our booking experience brings those essentials together. From a small household move to regular shop supplies or office equipment, each request starts with the route and the load, then moves to a suitable vehicle review.

We continue to refine the experience around useful information, transparent communication and a smoother hand-off from enquiry to delivery.

WHAT GUIDES US

Three principles behind every booking.

Useful details and clear communication help make transport requests easier to coordinate.

Clarity from the start

We ask for pickup, destination, load type and preferred timing so a request can be reviewed with the right context.

Practical vehicle choices

Vehicle categories help customers consider cargo space, load size and route access before confirming a trip.

Dependable coordination

Availability, final charges and pickup details should be reviewed before dispatch, keeping expectations clear for everyone.
