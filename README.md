# MoveFleet Premium v8

Responsive Mini Truck & Tempo Booking website.

## Public pages
- Home 1 and Home 2 — distinct homepage designs.
- About — company story, team roles and process.
- Services — service categories and animated booking flow.
- Fleet — compact, medium and larger vehicle guidance, including two detailed medium-load photo cards.
- Rate Chart — vehicle category guidance, quote factors, booking checklist and quote confirmation explanation.
- Contact — booking enquiry and service details.

## v8 refinements
- Fleet medium-load section now includes two photo cards and fuller descriptions for pickup trucks and mini trucks.
- Rate Chart explains vehicle categories, route distance, cargo size, loading, waiting, access and quote confirmation. It does not invent fixed prices.
- Navigation links have more breathing room, a visible active-page highlight and larger touch targets.
- Removed numeric prefixes from section eyebrow labels across all HTML pages.
- Existing dark/light theme, RTL/LTR, login/signup and customer dashboard are retained.

## Demo notes
Booking and account flows are frontend demonstrations. Connect a secure backend for real customer accounts, live bookings, GPS tracking, invoicing and payments. Replace demo contact details and business information before publishing.
